Export completed study data

Exports include confirmed complete sessions only. Unfinished uploads are not downloadable. The ZIP includes raw JSON checkpoints, CSV tables, sampling rates and plots. Check export_errors.csv for any receipts that need manual review.

Clear old incomplete study attempts

If someone closes the study before finishing, some of their gaze data may already have been saved. These attempts are not counted as completed studies and are not in your downloaded study results.

You can review and remove attempts with no completion confirmation and no activity for at least 96 hours (4 days). This is optional and is not automatic. Completed studies and records needing review are protected from this cleanup.

Find or delete a specific session

Use this section only when you need to find or remove one study attempt. It lists all saved sessions, including completed studies, unfinished attempts, and records requiring review. "Stored files" counts the pieces of data saved for a session; it is not the number of participants.

Deleting an individual session removes its stored study data. To prevent mistakes, you must enter the full internal session ID and your researcher password again. Download and check any data you need before deleting.

Delete entire session?

This removes the live stored study records, batches, and submission receipt for the selected session. It cannot be undone from this portal. Cloud storage retention policies may preserve recoverable copies. Consider exporting the data first.
